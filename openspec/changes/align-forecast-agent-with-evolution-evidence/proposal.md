## Why

The current forecast workbench freezes source records and supports persistent, typed agent execution, but its primary workflow predicts IPC fusion candidates and then generates theme-level narratives. Chapter 5 instead requires a fixed Chapter 4 evolution-evidence packet to constrain atomic development propositions that are independently challenged, engineering-reviewed, adjudicated, and selectively revised without treating agent agreement as evidence strength.

This change aligns the runnable agent architecture with that thesis method while keeping the evidence packet deliberately bounded: nine themes and their technical meaning, only the dynamic values and associations needed to support claims, source locations, mapping qualifications, optional representative patents, and explicit unknowns. It does not require a complete patent-paper knowledge graph or turn every currently empty field into a mandatory data-completion task.

## What Changes

- Add a versioned Chapter 4 evolution-evidence packet for `T01`–`T09` and optional `C01`–`C06` candidates, with typed theme facts, selected network trajectories, semantic/cooperation relations, source locations, mapping status, representative patents when available, and explicit evidentiary boundaries.
- Preserve the reported 926-document IPC-patent-topic mapping as an optional traceability attachment when supplied; absence of the underlying table is represented as unverified availability, not as proof that the research was not performed.
- Introduce stable, versioned atomic development propositions that distinguish historical observation, conditional extrapolation, target scenario, and insufficient evidence.
- Replace the single concept/critic sequence in the thesis workflow with five bounded responsibilities: evolution analysis, proposition generation, reverse review, engineering review, and evidence-based adjudication.
- Run reverse and engineering reviews independently, revise only affected propositions, recheck revised versions, and preserve unresolved objections when the bounded revision limit is reached.
- Separate deterministic reference/numeric validation from semantic support review, including checks for IPC-to-theme, static-to-dynamic, network-to-performance, and scenario-to-observation overreach.
- Replace model self-confidence and mandatory TRL output in the thesis workflow with evidence support levels, conditions, validation requirements, competing explanations, and unresolved gaps. Calibrated model probability remains a separate deterministic artifact where available.
- Generate per-theme conditional roadmaps and cross-theme synthesis only from adjudicated propositions, while carrying forward prerequisites and unresolved findings.
- Keep IPC-fusion discovery as a separate optional research mode whose reviewed outputs may enter the evidence packet as derived evidence but cannot redefine the nine thesis themes automatically.
- Add comparable single-agent, multi-agent, ablation, perturbation, cost, and stability evaluation contracts without claiming that the multi-agent method is superior before empirical runs exist.
- Preserve completed legacy jobs and readers. New thesis-aligned runs use research contract v3 and the 2010–2014, 2015–2019, and 2020–2025 windows.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `evolution-evidence-packets`: Build, validate, version, freeze, and trace the bounded Chapter 4 evidence context used by Chapter 5.
- `claim-centered-deliberation`: Generate and manage atomic development propositions through the five-role review, adjudication, local revision, termination, and roadmap workflow.
- `evidence-claim-validation`: Validate frozen references, copied numeric facts, object mappings, semantic support, inference boundaries, and evidence support levels.
- `forecast-method-evaluation`: Run fair single-agent, multi-agent, ablation, repeatability, perturbation, coverage, and resource-cost comparisons over the same frozen inputs.

### Modified Capabilities

No repository-level capability specifications currently exist. Legacy forecast and IPC-fusion behavior remains compatibility-scoped; the new specifications define the thesis-aligned path without rewriting completed artifacts.

## Impact

- Affects research contracts and schemas, evidence snapshots, stage-analysis adapters, LangGraph state and routing, prompt roles, validators, persistence tables, research APIs, result exports, and the project workbench review/results screens.
- Reuses the existing SQLite repository, research contract v3, deterministic network/model services, prompt/model manifests, checkpoints, human reviews, publication gates, and export provenance.
- Requires additive storage for evidence records, proposition versions, review findings, adjudications, and per-proposition terminal states.
- Deprecates the legacy topic confidence/mandatory-TRL semantics for new thesis-mode jobs without deleting or reinterpreting historical results.
